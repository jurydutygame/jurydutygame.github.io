Privacy Policy

Jury Duty · Last updated: November 2026

Jury Duty is a pass-and-play party game. We built it so that your game stays on your phone. The short version: we don't collect any of your data.

What stays on your device

Player names you type in, scores, game progress and which decks you've unlocked are saved only on your own device so your game survives if the app closes. This information is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app (or using "Erase everything" in the app's settings) removes it.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-ups
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No advertising
  • No selling or sharing of data
  • No servers that store your information

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple's handling of your information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Jury Duty is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
