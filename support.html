Jury Duty Support

Guilty until proven innocent.

How do I play?

Open the app and tap How to play on the home screen. In short: one player is accused, secretly pleads guilty or not guilty, and everyone else secretly predicts the plea.

I bought a deck but it's locked

Go to Decks on the home screen and tap Restore purchases at the bottom. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My game disappeared

Games are saved on your device automatically. If the app was deleted or "Erase everything" was used in settings, the saved game can't be recovered. Your purchased decks can always be restored.
